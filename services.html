Our Support Services

Comprehensive, person-centered care that respects dignity, promotes independence, and enhances quality of life for every individual we serve.

At KIRH, we understand that quality care goes beyond meeting basic needs. Our holistic approach encompasses physical, emotional, and social well-being, delivered by trained professionals who genuinely care about the people they serve. Every service is customized to honor individual preferences, goals, and aspirations.

Core Care Services

Personal & Daily Living Support

Essential daily care services delivered with compassion, respect, and attention to individual dignity.

🛁

Bathing & Grooming

Dignified personal hygiene support tailored to individual needs and preferences.

  • •Assistance with bathing and showering
  • •Hair care and grooming
  • •Oral hygiene support
  • •Dressing assistance
❤️

Meal Support

Nutritious meal planning, preparation, and feeding assistance to maintain health and well-being.

  • •Meal planning and preparation
  • •Dietary requirement management
  • •Feeding assistance
  • •Nutrition education
💊

Medication Reminders

Reliable medication management to ensure health protocols are followed consistently and safely.

  • •Medication schedule tracking
  • •Dosage reminders
  • •Prescription coordination
👁

Safe Supervision

Compassionate oversight and safety monitoring in a respectful, non-intrusive manner.

  • •24/7 safety monitoring available
  • •Fall prevention strategies
  • •Emergency response protocols
