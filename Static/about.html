Our Story

Founded on Dignity, Driven by Impact

Kenya Industrial Research Homes (KIRH) was established with a powerful vision: to transform the landscape of care and opportunity for Persons with Disabilities in Kenya. We recognized that true empowerment requires more than basic support—it demands holistic care, skill development, and genuine pathways to independence.

Through strong partnerships with government ministries, including the Ministry of Education, Health, and Social Protection, the National Council for Persons with Disabilities (NCPWD), County governments, and dedicated disability organizations, we've built a comprehensive ecosystem of support.

Today, KIRH stands as a beacon of hope and progress, continuously championing for a disability-inclusive society where youth with disabilities are respected, empowered, and supported to develop their full potential and lead quality, dignified lives.

Our Vision

To create environments where care meets opportunity, and every person thrives with dignity, independence, and access to innovation.

Our Mission

To deliver holistic, accessible care while empowering PWDs through training, leadership development, and technology integration—promoting an inclusive society where persons with different abilities have access to affordable quality training services and become self-reliant.
